# MDV_coral_scale
Assessment of the variation of δ13C and δ15N isotopes in *Pocillopora verrucosa* across reef type, site, between colony, and fragment scale. Samples were collected in North Male' atoll, Maldives in November 2025.

Maldives research permit number: NRP2025/87
Maldives CITES export permit numbers: 25-MV/0006/E06; 25-MV/007/E07
